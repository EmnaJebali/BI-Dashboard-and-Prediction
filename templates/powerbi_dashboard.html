To access your Power BI dashboard, please configure the embed URL:

Option 1: Set the POWERBI_EMBED_URL in airline/settings.py
Option 2: Set the POWERBI_EMBED_URL environment variable

How to get your Power BI embed URL:
1. Open your Power BI report in Power BI Service
2. Click "File" → "Embed report" → "Website or portal"
3. Copy the embed URL and paste it in your settings

Note: The URL should look like:
https://app.powerbi.com/reportEmbed?reportId=YOUR_REPORT_ID&autoAuth=true&ctid=YOUR_TENANT_ID
